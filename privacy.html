Privacy policy: claude-os mini

Last updated 23 October 2026.

Who uses it

Only its owner. It is a private, single-user app with no other users.

What it accesses

The owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar, with read-only permissions only (gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly). It cannot send, change, label or delete anything.

How the data is used

A script on the owner's own computer reads recent mail and calendar entries to prepare a daily summary for the owner. That content is processed by an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) solely to produce this summary for the owner. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Storage and removal

Summaries are kept on the owner's own devices. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

The owner, through the GitHub account that hosts this page.
